**Assignment: Seller Earnings & Sold Products Notification System**

**Task Description:**

You need to implement a feature in the project with the following requirements:

1. **Product Selling Flow**

   * A seller adds products for sale.
   * These products are displayed in the customer panel/table.
   * A customer can purchase and confirm an order.

2. **Seller Notification**

   * When a customer buys a product, the product owner (seller) must be notified that:

     > “Your product has been sold.”

3. **Earnings Section for Seller**

   * After login, each seller must have a new menu option called **“Earnings.”**
   * In this section, the seller should see:

     * List of their sold products only
     * Customer name who bought the product
     * Quantity sold`
     * Date and time of purchase
     * Total earnings from each sale

4. **Important Rules**

   * Each seller can see **only their own sold products and earnings.**
   * Sellers must NOT see other sellers’ sales data.

**Goal:**
Create a complete system where sellers can track their product sales and earnings after customers place confirmed orders.
